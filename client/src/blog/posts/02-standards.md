# Why Data Standards Matter for Democracy

Civic data fragmentation isn't just a technical problem. It's a democratic one.

When representative data, election information, and district boundaries aren't standardized across jurisdictions, voters face friction at every turn:

- Can't easily find who represents them
- Can't access contact information
- Can't compare voting records across districts
- Can't hold officials accountable

## The Standards Solution

By establishing shared data standards, we ensure interoperability, accessibility, auditability, and sustainability across the civic data ecosystem.

Civic Data Tech is convening practitioners, government partners, and community organizations to develop standards for:

- Representative records
- District boundaries
- Election information
- Government structure

These standards will become the de facto practice in civic tech—adopted by default, not as an afterthought.
