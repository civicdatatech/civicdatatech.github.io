# Welcome to Civic Data Tech

We're excited to announce the launch of Civic Data Tech, a membership association / incubator (tbc on the business model!) dedicated to supporting open-source civic data projects and maintainers.

## Our Mission

Civic data infrastructure is fragmented. Projects operate in silos. Standards are inconsistent. **We're here to change that.**

Civic Data Tech brings together practitioners, maintainers, and organizations to:

- Establish shared data standards for representative data, election information, and government structure
- Provide startup support and funding for emerging civic data initiatives
- Sustain critical open-source projects through grants and mentorship
- Advocate for data accessibility and transparency at all levels of government

## Why Now?

Voters can't easily find and contact their elected officials. Communities lack access to consistent civic data. Maintainers work without adequate support or resources.

**This is a democratic problem, and it requires a community solution.**

## What's Next

We're in the build phase for both community and product. We hold weekly check in calls and bi monthly hackathons. Come join us [Unified](https://unified.me/organization/27721/posts?invite=civicdata) to learn more.
