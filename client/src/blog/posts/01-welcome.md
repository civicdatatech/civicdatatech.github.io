# Welcome to Civic Data Tech

We're excited to announce the launch of Civic Data Tech, a collaborative community dedicated to supporting the maintainers and projects that power civic data infrastructure nationwide.

## Our Mission

Civic data infrastructure is fragmented. Projects operate in silos. Standards are inconsistent. Maintainers work without adequate support or resources. **We're here to change that.**

Civic Data Tech brings together practitioners, maintainers, and organizations to:

- Support the teams and projects maintaining critical civic data infrastructure
- Establish shared data standards that work across all levels of government
- Focus on local government data—the layer most civic tech infrastructure neglects
- Provide funding, mentorship, and community support to sustain these projects
- Advocate for data accessibility and transparency at all levels of government

## Why Now?

Voters can't easily find and contact their elected officials. Communities lack access to consistent civic data. Maintainers work without adequate support or resources.

While state and federal civic data infrastructure is comparatively well-covered, local government data remains fragmented and underfunded. The teams maintaining these critical projects—the civic tech maintainers—are stretched thin.

**This is a structural problem, and it requires a community solution.**

## What's Next

We're in the build phase for both community and product. We hold weekly check in calls and bi monthly hackathons. Come join us [Unified](https://unified.me/organization/27721/posts?invite=civicdata) to learn more.
